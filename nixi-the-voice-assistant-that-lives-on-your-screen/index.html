Will it run on my PC?

Slide in your specs.

Start with the built-in model. Explore larger options with the same memory estimates used in Settings.

Required

64-bit Windows 10 or 11 and a working microphone. Keep at least 4 GB free for setup and updates.

Memory

8 GB RAM is our starting recommendation. 16 GB gives more room for your other apps. An exact minimum CPU and RAM configuration is still being validated.

No graphics card needed

Default speech recognition runs on the CPU. A compatible graphics card can speed up AI writing. Older PCs may take longer.

Start with the built-in model.

A dedicated graphics card is optional. AI writing can be slower on older processors, especially while other apps are busy.

What does a larger model change?

It can improve AI drafts, Polished dictation and text-based reasoning. It does not upgrade speech recognition or add new commands. Larger models need more memory and may respond more slowly.

Optional models are listed in Settings → AI model. The 2B model is the first upgrade to consider; 4B, 9B and 27B are beta options. Model downloads need extra disk space.

Keep the built-in model for screenshot understanding. Larger downloads currently do not include the matching vision component. The model download and switching flow is being checked before we recommend upgrading.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

What can Nixi actually do?

Open apps and websites, control volume and music, set timers and reminders, keep your to-do list and notes, find your files, answer questions about your screen, run a teleprompter, dictate into any app and write first drafts. It handles a defined set of everyday actions, and it always asks before anything risky.

Does it need the internet?

Core features work offline after setup: listening, dictation, commands and AI writing. Opening websites, searching the web, updates and model downloads need an internet connection.

Is it really free?

Everything in Nixi’s free version will always stay free, including dictation. A future subscription may add upgraded features or more computing power. We ask for your email for beta access and product updates.

Will my PC run it?

You need 64-bit Windows 10 or 11, a microphone and at least 4 GB free for setup and updates. Start with 8 GB RAM; 16 GB leaves more room for other apps. These are recommendations, not a validated minimum hardware specification. A dedicated graphics card is optional. Older processors can take longer to transcribe or generate text. See Your PC above.

Will a bigger AI model improve dictation?

It can improve Polished mode and AI writing, but it does not change the speech-recognition model or unlock new commands. Bigger models use more memory and may be slower. Optional model downloads are in Settings → AI model. Keep the built-in model for screenshot understanding; larger downloads currently lack its matching vision component.

Is it always listening?

With the wake phrase on, heynixi checks speech on your PC while the app is running. Audio is not saved or sent to an AI service. Turn wake detection off in Settings and start Command with a button instead.

Which languages does it speak?

English for now.

Is there a Mac version?

Mac dictation is in development. Leave your email when you request the Windows beta and we'll share an update when it's ready.
